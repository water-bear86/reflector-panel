## 2026-07-13 - Comprehensive accessibility for pipeline creation
**Learning:** Modern dApps often neglect basic accessibility in favor of "dazzle" effects. Standard HTML/ARIA practices like `htmlFor`/`id` linking, `aria-describedby` for hints, and `aria-pressed` for custom toggles are essential even in highly stylized interfaces.
**Action:** Always verify that every input has an accessible name (label or aria-label) and that selection states in custom components are programmatically exposed.
