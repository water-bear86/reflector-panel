## 2024-05-22 - [Form and Icon-only Link Accessibility]
**Learning:** In highly visual interfaces like crypto dashboards, form labels and icon-only buttons are often neglected for accessibility. Using `htmlFor`/`id` for labels and `aria-label` for icon buttons significantly improves screen reader support without affecting the visual design.
**Action:** Always check for missing label associations and `aria-label` on social links or action icons.
